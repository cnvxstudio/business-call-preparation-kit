# Business Call Preparation Kit

A practical checklist and reusable templates for small teams handling customer enquiries by phone.

I help build [Frontel](https://aiphoneansweringservice.io/), an AI phone answering service for businesses. This independent resource helps teams prepare clear business information and follow up on incoming calls. You can use the templates with your own reception workflow.

## 1. Prepare the information callers need

- Business name and the services you offer.
- Opening days, hours, and your business time zone.
- Public address, directions, parking, or accessibility details, when relevant.
- Prices or starting prices your team is comfortable sharing.
- Policies and common questions, including when staff should follow up.
- The details your team needs for a callback or appointment request.

Keep these details current and include only information intended for callers. Leave out passwords, internal notes, payment details, and sensitive customer information.

## 2. Example business information

**Fictional example — Green Bike Shop**

```text
Services: Bike repairs and tire changes.
Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
Time zone: America/Los_Angeles.
Pricing: Tire changes start at $20. Staff confirm the final price.
Parking: Free parking is behind the shop.
Appointments: Record the requested service, preferred time, name,
and callback number. Staff check availability and confirm the appointment.
If a question is not covered here, take a message for the team.
```

Use a business's actual hours, prices, policies, and time zone. Do not copy the example as though it describes your own business.

## 3. Call note template

```text
Call received: [date, time, business time zone]
Caller name: [name supplied by caller]
Callback number: [number confirmed with caller]
Reason for calling: [short factual summary]
Requested service: [service, if relevant]
Preferred appointment time: [requested date/time and time zone]
Question for the team: [unanswered question, if any]
Next step: [callback / check availability / provide information]
Owner: [team member]
Status: [needs follow-up / awaiting caller / handled]
```

A requested appointment is a preference for staff to review. It is not a confirmed booking until the business has checked availability and confirmed it with the caller.

## 4. Follow-up checklist

1. Check the caller's request and contact details.
2. Verify any availability, price, or policy that needs staff confirmation.
3. Contact the caller with the answer or next step.
4. Record the outcome and mark the note as handled when appropriate.
